# Suggested 14-slide Presentation (12-minute presentation + demo)

1. Title — Saffron & Slate Restaurant Management System, student details.
2. Domain background — restaurant record-management challenges.
3. Problem statement and objectives.
4. Functional requirements and main modules.
5. Technology stack — React/Vite, Node/Express, MySQL, mysql2.
6. System architecture and data flow.
7. ER diagram — eight entities and cardinalities.
8. Relational schema — PKs, FKs and constraints.
9. Normalization — 1NF, 2NF and 3NF.
10. Backend implementation — REST endpoints, parameterized queries and error handling.
11. SQL demonstration — JOIN, nested query, aggregates, GROUP BY/HAVING and three views.
12. Frontend demonstration — forms and CRUD workflows.
13. Reports, search and testing — sample data, validation and report screenshots.
14. Conclusion and future enhancements.

Suggested timing: 1 minute introduction/problem; 2 minutes requirements/architecture; 2 minutes database/ERD/normalization; 2 minutes backend/SQL; 3 minutes live demo; 1 minute testing/reports; 1 minute conclusion and questions.
